\subsection*{Definitions used in the charts}
Latency is the recorded client duration in milliseconds. Percentiles use nearest rank: for sorted observations $x_{(1)},\ldots,x_{(n)}$, $P_p=x_{(\lceil pn\rceil)}$. Thus the median for 100 calls is the 50th ordered duration, not the average of the 50th and 51st.

Completion rate equals $100/(11946/1000)=8.3710$ calls/s. Its denominator includes pacing and connection setup. It is not the configured offered rate or a measured maximum capacity.

\Limit{The JSON has batch indices but no per-call or batch-start timestamps. Counts and percentiles can be recomputed. Concurrent overlap and the reported pacing cannot be reconstructed independently from these fields.}
