\subsection*{The response-mapping failure}
The failing operation is \texttt{GET /integration/echo/last}. Its expected \texttt{data} object uses \texttt{allOf} and \texttt{nullable}, outside the declared historical subset. The generator emitted \texttt{responsePath: data} without an output schema. The profile audit calls this an \textbf{eligibility-oracle inconsistency and response-mapping limitation}. It is neither a supported-pass nor a corrected rerun. [S6]

\KeyPoint{Preserve the denominator}{State ``7 of 8 historical probe rows passed all structural checks.'' Do not turn this into a 7/7 supported-subset claim or general 87.5\% API-conversion accuracy.}
