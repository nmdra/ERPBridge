\subsection*{Discovery/version consistency case}
Two descriptive rows record reverse registration order, \texttt{v2\_then\_v1}. Before the fix, discovery omitted a v2-only argument although resolution selected version 2.0.0. After the fix, discovery included the argument and the recorded agreement flag became true. [S12]

\begin{table}[h]\caption{Recorded discovery/resolution agreement}\EvidenceTableStart
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}Xccc@{}}\toprule
Record & v2 argument shown & Resolved version & Agreement\\\midrule
Before fix & No & 2.0.0 & False\\
After fix & Yes & 2.0.0 & True\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}\end{table}

The pre-fix row records HTTP 200 for a v2-only-argument call. The post-fix invocation field is blank. A blank is missing evidence, not proof of a successful post-fix invocation. This is a useful defect-and-repair example, not a complete regression suite.

\Limit{Do not pool separate experiments, convert sparse summaries into a capacity claim, or interpret absent post-fix measurements as passes. The report distinguishes a recorded consistency repair from complete per-call revision safety.}
